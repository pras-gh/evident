05 — Straight answers

Before you ask.

How do I know it isn’t making things up?

Because you never have to take its word for it. Every sentence carries a citation to a specific passage, and the passage is highlighted in the original document. If a claim has no source, it doesn’t ship.

What’s actually covered?

10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy statements, earnings call transcripts and investor presentations for 5,000+ US-listed companies, going back roughly twenty years.

How fresh is the data?

New filings are indexed as they are published to EDGAR. Transcripts land shortly after each call ends.

Can I export an answer?

Yes — answers export with their citations intact, so the evidence travels with the memo instead of getting lost in a copy-paste.

Is this investment advice?

No. Ellivate is a research tool. It finds and cites what companies have said in public; the judgement stays yours.
